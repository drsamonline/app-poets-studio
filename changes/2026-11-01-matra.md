A ghazal written in Devanagari or Gujarati is now measured in **matra**, not in English
phonemes: every line of a couplet must share the same count, which is the practical test
of a shared beher. The count is shown in the gutter and the script is named under the
form. Words in any script are now split correctly — the word breaker kept only A-Z, so a
Hindi line arrived as no words at all.
